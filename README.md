# FocusRusher

**An all-in-one study productivity platform that helps students stay focused, organize their academic life, and collaborate with other learners.**

## Overview

FocusRusher is a student-focused productivity platform designed to make studying more organized, engaging, and collaborative.

Beyond traditional productivity tools, FocusRusher brings together time management, academic tracking, and a student community in one place.

The platform was developed beyond the prototype stage and reached real users. It also participated in a state-level competition, providing an opportunity to present the project in a competitive environment.

## Key Features

### Focus & Productivity
- **Focus Timer:** Structured study sessions designed to improve concentration and reduce distractions.
- **Task Management:** Organize academic responsibilities and keep track of priorities.
- **Productivity Statistics:** Monitor study activity and visualize personal progress.
- **Leaderboard:** A competitive feature that encourages consistency and engagement.

### Student Community

A social learning space where students can connect, collaborate, and help each other.

- **Student Discussions:** Share questions, exchange ideas, and discuss academic topics.
- **Resource Sharing:** Upload and share educational materials, including images, PDFs, and other learning resources.
- **Peer Support:** Help other students solve doubts and learn collaboratively.
- **Community Interaction:** Participate in discussions and engage with other learners.

### Authentication
- Email and password authentication.
- Anonymous authentication.
- Powered by Firebase Authentication.
